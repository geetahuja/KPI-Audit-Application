# Reference discovery — 7 October 2026

All six supplied workbooks were inspected read-only before application implementation. The filename supplied for Entech MCLS does not contain `(1)`; it is the Entech MCLS reference used here.

## Workbook classification and sheet roles

Each company supplies a Master calculation workbook and an MCLS input/management workbook. Sheet roles are derived from names and content. Data Add Sheet is the operational input layer; Productivity, ROI, ITR, Quality, OTD and OEE sheets calculate downstream KPIs; Dashboard, Benchmark, Evidence and Graph sheets are consumers. Company Information and Formulae contain configuration; responsibility/activity sheets are management records. Additional and renamed sheets are legitimate variations.

Full sheet inventories, dimensions, visibility, merges, named ranges, charts, validation counts, formula counts, cross-sheet dependencies and conversion-artifact counts are generated by `scripts/inspect_workbooks.py`. Raw workbook exports and confidential source workbooks are deliberately excluded from Git.

## Derived business rules

* Productivity 1: material = sales × material percentage / 100; throughput = sales − material; operating expenses = salaries + wages + energy + fuel + consumables + maintenance + outsourcing + other expenses; productivity = throughput / expenses.
* Productivity 2: material consumption = opening stock + receipts − closing stock; throughput = sales − material consumption. The result row differs across Entech, Advent and R&D. Match row meaning, not coordinates.
* ROI: net profit / (capital + inventory). Entech ROI 1 is a ratio; ROI 2 multiplies by 100. Preserve variants and surface contradictory labels for review.
* ITR: throughput / total month-end inventory, including dead stock. Do not substitute a textbook sales/average-stock definition.
* Quality: rejected / produced; PPM adds × 1,000,000. Customer quality uses returns / sold.
* OTD: on-time orders / total due × 100. YTD uses cumulative numerator and denominator.
* OEE: availability, performance and quality each expressed as percentages; final OEE requires division by 10,000 when multiplying those percentages.
* Kaizen: manual + machine + process counts. Savings is a separate currency KPI.
* Savings: sum six categories. Floor area uses square metres. HR record man-hours are people × hours where those columns exist. Lead time record duration is shipment date − order date.

## Governance and unresolved reference questions

The designated clean reference contains `__xludf.DUMMYFUNCTION`, `IMPORTRANGE`, cached fallback values, and intentional graph `NA()` gaps. These are not automatically business errors. Imported cached data cannot independently verify the originating source. Upload matching MCLS and Master together to reconnect matching source fields and periods locally.

Some optional expense fields are blank or annotated as included in material cost. Missing optional expense amounts are not silently classified as mandatory input failures. A calculation relying on them must state its zero/blank policy. All-zero OEE inputs require applicability review, not six critical errors.

Reference formula patterns are candidates; approval controls enforcement. A differing formula is a review item until its meaning or recalculated result proves an error. Company targets are not inherited from Entech.

## Validation and reconciliation cases

1. Six-workbook read-only regression: parse every sheet; preserve input hashes; classify Master versus MCLS; identify technical conversions separately.
2. Shift a calculation row while retaining labels: same semantic dependencies, no coordinate-difference error.
3. Rejects greater than production, or on-time orders greater than due: data error with downstream impact.
4. Replace OTD denominator with production: semantic formula review; independent recalculation establishes a KPI error when source data are available.
5. Replace a KPI formula with a number: formula-governance error even if the number is currently correct.
6. Unequal monthly order volumes: ratio-of-totals YTD differs from average monthly percentages.
7. Unsupported or external functions: blocked verification, never a passed check.
8. Missing numerator, zero denominator, duplicate period, malformed ZIP/XML and oversized input: explicitly handled.
9. Negative delivery duration and inconsistent HR man-hours: record-level data/reconciliation errors.
10. Company-specific target, disabled KPI or threshold: use that company configuration only.

## Architecture decision

The hosted MVP processes files in the browser: no workbook upload, AI transmission or server storage. This gives confidential workbooks a smaller exposure surface and works within Sites hosting. A standalone FastAPI adapter and container are included for server-side integrations, using openpyxl parsing and the same deterministic JavaScript engine, avoiding two implementations of financial arithmetic. PostgreSQL persistence, multi-user business roles and managed encrypted archival storage are a future production deployment layer, not simulated controls in the browser. The hosted Site uses owner-private access. Browser history is opt-in and can be deleted; no password or fake login screen is supplied.
